Physical context has always required retention. That's why this quadrant is empty. The two purpose-built recorders of this generation, Humane and Limitless, are already dead or absorbed. Trace's architecture is the first way in.
Why won't Apple or Google just build this?

Their business models monetize retained data and engagement; their privacy stack deliberately stops at your digital life. A zero-retention physical layer contradicts their data economics. If the giants want this capability, they license it or acquire it. Both outcomes require Trace to exist first.

The device wins adoption. The layer earns the money.
